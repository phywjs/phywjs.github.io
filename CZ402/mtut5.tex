\problem Consider a set E generated from $1$, $x$, $e^x$, and $\log x$ by
addition, subtraction and multiplication. For examples, $1 + 3 x$,
$e^{2x} - x$, or $x \log x$ belong to E. 
What is the algebraic domain (ring? integral domain? or field?) of E? 

\problem The set G of {\sl Gaussian integers} is the subset of
the complex number C defined by
$$ {\rm G} = \{ a + b \sqrt{-1}: a, b \in {\rm Z}\}$$
\item{} (where we usually use the notation $\sqrt{-1} = i$, and Z
is the set of all integers).  
\itemitem{(a)} Verify that G,
with the standard operations of addition and multiplication of complex
numbers, is an integral domain.  Further, verify that G is a Euclidean
domain with the valuation
$$ v(a+b\sqrt{-1}) = a^2 + b^2.$$
Describe (propose) a way to compute $q$ and $r$ given $a$ and $b$ such
that
$$a = bq + r, \quad v(r) < v(b), \quad a, b, q, r \in {\rm G}$$.
\itemitem{(b)} What are the units in G?  (Units are elements that
have inverses). 
Give some examples of primes in G. Given some examples of associates.
(If $a|b$ and $b|a$, $a$ and $b$ are called associates).

\problem 
\itemitem{(a)} Apply Algorithm 2.1 (Euclidean algorithm) 
by hand to compute, in the Euclidean domain Q[$x$] (i.e., polynomial
with rational numbers as coefficients),
$$ {\rm GCD}(4x^4+13x^3+15x^2+7x+1,\; 2x^3+x^2-4x-3).$$
\itemitem{(b)} Repeat with extended Euclidean algorithm. Find $s(x)$ and
$t(x)$ such that ${\rm GCD}(a(x),b(x)) = a(x)s(x) + b(x)t(x)$.
\itemitem{(c)} Repeat the calculation in part (a) but in Z$_5[x]$.
