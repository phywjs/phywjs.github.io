\problem Define a Lisp function {\tt infix} that converts a Restricted 
Algebraic Expression (RAE) from prefix notation to infix notation.  
RAE is either the form {\tt ($op$ $x$ $y$)} or {\tt ($f$ $x$)}, where
$x$ and $y$ are again RAE or symbols, and $op$ is an operator, and 
$f$ is the name of a function.  The infix form is obtained by simply changing
every occurrence of {\tt ($op$ $x$ $y$)} to {\tt ($x$ $op$ $y$)},
for examples, 

\ll{\h (infix '(+ 2 x)) $\quad\Rightarrow\quad$ (2 + x)}
\ll{\h (infix '(+ 2 (* x y))) $\quad\Rightarrow\quad$ (2 + (x * y))}
\ll{\h (infix '(\char`\^\ (sin x) -1)) $\quad\Rightarrow\quad$ ((sin x) \char`\^\ -1)}
\bye
