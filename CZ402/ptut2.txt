\problem Elaborate on Exercise 3, Question 1.
Work out the steps leading to the result for least-square fit 
$$ A^T A c = A^T y.$$

\problem  After specifying the following assignments and rules,
\ttbg
f[2] = 3
f[u] := 2u^2
f[v_] := 1/v
u = 2
\tted
what is {\tt f[u]}.

(defun mystery (s)
   (cond ((null s) 1)
         ((atom s) 0)
         (t (max (+ (mystery (first s)) 1)
                    (mystery (rest s))))))
