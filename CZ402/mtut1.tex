\problem Give a method to represent floating point number with
arbitrary accuracy (say in C), and algorithms for addition, 
substraction, multiplication, and division of the arbitrary accuracy
floating point numbers.

\problem  What is the {\sl Head} of the following mathematica expressions:
{\tt 3}, {\tt 1/2}, {\tt 1.0000}, {\tt Pi}, {\tt I}, 
{\tt \char`\{a,b,c\char`\}},
{\tt "string"}, {\tt a[0]}, {\tt h[a,b,c]}, {\tt a + b}, {\tt x\char`\^2}?
Why?

\problem Discuss the similarity and differences of ordinary assignment
{\tt =} (also known as {\tt Set[]}) and delayed assignment {\tt :=}
(also known as {\tt SetDelayed[]}).  Give an example where you can
see the different effect.
