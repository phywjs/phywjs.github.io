Quantum Thermal Transport and NEGF

We post here some of the quantum thermal transport related codes, articles, theses.

The review article here, or arxiv version, or at the publisher Eur. Phys. J. B. {62}, 381 (2008). A follow-up review is at arXiv:1303.7317. A new review on "electron-photon systems" here or at FOP.

Students theses:

  • Zeng Nan's PhD thesis (basic NEGF),
  • Imam Makhfudz's MSc thesis (spin waves),
  • Zhang Lifa's PhD thesis (phonon Hall effect),
  • Leek Meng Lee's PhD thesis (rigorous NEGF),
  • Bijay Kumar Agarwalla's PhD thesis (full counting statistics),
  • Juzar Thingna's PhD thesis (quantum master equation),
  • Li Huanan's PhD thesis (consistent quantum histories/FCS),
  • Qiu Hongfei's PhD thesis (Oxides EuTiO3),
  • Chen Ruofan's PhD thesis (dynamic mean fields),
  • Peng Jiebin's PhD thesis (near-field radiative heat transfer),
  • Yap Han Hoe's PhD thesis (Floquet NEGF, topological phases),
  • Sun Kangtai's PhD thesis (phonon Hall effect).
  • Ren Yuhua's Honours project (electron in B field).

ballistic-transmission.nb, Mathematica modules for various algorithms of ballistic transmission functions discussed in the review. noneq.nb, for 1D nonlinear systems, etc.

balli.tar.gz, an MPI Fortran 90 implementation of the Caroli formula for ballistic phonon transmission. qmd.tar.gz, a 1D quantum molecular dynamics code (C and Fortran 90, need fftw3).

A Mathematica code FeynmanT4.nb to generate Feynman diagrams for phonon nonequilibrium Green's functions. You can still read the code with Mathematica Player if you don't have the software to run.
