Ferro-electric phase transitions from first principles

Many perovskites such as BaTiO3 has para to ferro-electric phase transitions. The theories of such transitions, such as that based on Landau theory is long known. However, first-principles based calculations are very few. In this project, we consider the para to ferro phase transition about 400K in BaTiO3, based on so-called effective phonon theory. In this theory we compute dielectric functon and ``order parameter'' by a self consistent iterations. Hopefully, the theory is general enough so that above and below Tc can be described correctly. The student is expected to have a good foundation in quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, solid state physics, and computer skills, such as C/C++. This is theoretical/computational project.
