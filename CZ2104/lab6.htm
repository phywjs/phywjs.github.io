Part 1

 

In Lab 5, you have programmed the temple with wire frame and as solid polygons with interactive viewing.

 

Add light effect by putting one or two point like source and material properties of the objects.   Experiment with the values of parameters of light source and material properties.

 

The polygons should have the same (intrinsic) uniform color of your choice.  Then you should modify your program to have the normal of each surface properly defined.  Define the light source and material property.

 

1.: hand in the code. Your code should be carefully commented.  Also keep your soft copy in your directory for TA to inspect.  Same for Part 2.

 

Read the chapter on light and material interaction for details.  See also the teapot example.
