Parameterized Macros

    #define PRINT3(e1,e2,e3) \


    printf("\n%c\t%c\t%d", \

    (e1), (e2), (e3) )

  • After this definition, the preprocessor replaces every occurrence of the form

      PRINT3(a,b,c)

    by the replacement text

    printf("\n%c\t%c\t%d",

    (a), (b), (c) )
