Real World Applications: Computing Resistance

  • Problem

    • Given single-character codes for the colored bands that mark a resistor, compute its resistance in ohms. The color codes are as follows:

      Black 0, Brown 1,
      Red 2, Orange 3, Yellow 4,
      Green 5, Blue 6,
      Violet 7,Gray 8, White 9.
    • If the integer codes of the bands are (in order) c1, c2, and c3, the resistance in ohms is
      R = (10*c1+c2)*10c3
