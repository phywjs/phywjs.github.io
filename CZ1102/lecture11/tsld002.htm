Sample Input/Output

  • What is the resistance for a resistor with color mark ?

    The colored bands are coded as follows:

    COLOR CODE

    ----- ----

    Black--------> B

    Brown--------> N

    Red----------> R

    Orange-------> O

    Yellow-------> Y

    Green--------> G

    Blue---------> E

    Violet-------> V

    Gray---------> A

    White--------> W

    Enter three codes. ERO

    Resistance in ohms: 62000.000000
