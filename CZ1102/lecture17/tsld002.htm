Command-Line

  • If a program with argc and argv declared is compiled with executable called a.out, then after

      a.out file1 5

  • the two parameters, argc and argv[], will contain following values when the program starts.

    argc

    3

    argv

    argv[0]

    argv[1]

    argv[2]

    "a.out"

    "file1"

    "5"
