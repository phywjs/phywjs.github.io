Use pointer without Initialization is an error

    char *s;


    scanf("%s", s); Error: pointer has no value!

  • Correct one should be:

    char *s;

    char str[10];

    s = str;

    scanf("%s", s);

    or scanf("%s", str);

    or scanf("%s", &str[0]);

  • But

    scanf("%s", &s);

    is wrong.
