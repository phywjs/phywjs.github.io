Array Index

  • The index of a 10-cell array varies from 0 to 9. So

    float temps[10];

  • defined 10 cells. We refer to these 10 cells as

      temps[0], temps[1],

      temps[2], temps[3],
      . . . , temps[7],
      temps[8], temps[9].

  • A reference to temps[10] is a programming error (and difficult to detect).
