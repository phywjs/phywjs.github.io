Format Descriptor %s

    scanf("%s", stooge2);

  • The conversion specification %s read a sequence of characters, with initial white space skipped, up to the next (but not include) white space. A terminating '\0' is added to the sequence.

  • A white space character is either return, or space, or tab, etc.

  • E.g., if input is

      Curly?

      stooge2 array becomes

    C

    u

    r

    l

    y

    \0
