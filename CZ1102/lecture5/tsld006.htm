Association

  • Operators *, /, and % have the save precedence; operator + and - have the same precedence. They all associates from left to right.

  • Thus

      5 - 3 + 4 is

      (5 - 3) + 4 = 2 + 4 = 6

      8 % 3 * 5 is
      (8 % 3) * 5 = 2 * 5 = 10
