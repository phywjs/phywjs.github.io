Honours Research Projects (2010)

Surface Green's function and self-energy for two-dimensional leads in heat transport

The surface Green's function and self-energy are important quantities to know in a ballistic heat transport calculation. In this project, we first learn the method of the nonequilibrium Green's function (NEGF), and then focus our attention for the calculation of the surface Green's functions. After working out the result for a 1D harmonic spring chain, we intend to work out, if possible, analytically, the self-energy of the 1D finite chain connected to a 2D square lattice. If time allows, we may go into numerical calculation of graphene strips connected to an semi-infinite half plane of graphene. These calculations will be relevant to the thermal transport studies both by NEGF and molecular dynamics simulation with quantum baths.

Good background in statistical mechanics and quantum mechanics is required. For beginning reading, see J.-S. Wang, J. Wang, and J. T. Lu, Eur. Phys. J. B, 62, 381 (2008).

The project is theoretical/computational.
