Postdoctoral Research Fellow

One Research Fellow position is available starting 
from January 2023 in Prof. Jian-Sheng Wang's group at National 
University of Singapore.  

The research will be on energy transfer and Casimir force in 
nonequilibrium electron-photon systems.  This project is to work 
on the transport of energy, momentum, and angular momentum mediated by 
photons between objects with microscopic models. 
We intend to perform three kinds of calculations, 1) the transfer of 
energy between nanostructures, such as flat sheets
in side by side geometry, 2) Casimir forces in carbon systems 
(graphene, nanotubes, buckyballs), 3) strong electromagnetic fields and 
nonlinear effects.  A good background in many-body theories, 
good programming skills are expected.  Knowledge of quantum electrodynamics 
is an advantage. 

The appointment will be initially for one year and can be extended up to 
three years or project ending date.  The applicants should submit by email 
a complete curriculum vitae, and ask three letters of references sent directly.
